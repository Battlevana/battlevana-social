NEW COMBO
CommanderDarkhold Destiny · 9 Apr 2027
GOOD TO KNOW
  • Not instant. Both Darkhold abilities need a tap and the finisher is sorcery speed, so the combo plays out over several turns.
  • Protect the Angel. If Platinum Angel leaves the battlefield while you are at 0 or below, you lose immediately.
  • Backup plan. Repay in Kind has the same effect as the finisher, giving the deck a second way to close.
  • Colors. Mono-black plus a colorless artifact, so it fits any Commander deck with black in it.
  • When. The Darkhold becomes legal when Marvel: Darkhold Destiny releases on 9 April 2027.
