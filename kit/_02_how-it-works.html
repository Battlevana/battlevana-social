NEW COMBO
CommanderDarkhold Destiny · 9 Apr 2027
HOW IT WORKS
1
Get Platinum Angel on the battlefield
You can't lose the game, even at 0 life or lower.
2
Tap The Darkhold, turn after turn
Draw a card for each sin counter, then lose 1 life for each card in your hand. Keep going until your life is 0 or below.
3
Pay 6BB and tap The Darkhold
Every player's life total becomes the lowest one: yours. Your opponents drop to 0 or less and lose the game.
